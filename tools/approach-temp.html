What a few kelvin of approach costs you.

Work backwards from the coldest water the chips need to the warmest water the facility may supply. Every kelvin of approach across the CDU, and every kelvin across the dry cooler, pushes the required outdoor temperature down and takes free-cooling hours with it.

°C

ASHRAE liquid-cooling classes W32, W40 and W45 set an upper facility-water-supply limit of 32, 40 and 45 °C for equipment declared to that class; they are operating envelopes, not set-points. Enter the supply your racks actually require.

4 K

Include the fouling allowance. Datasheet approach is usually clean-plate.

8 K

Illustrative dry-bulb distributions only, shaped from typical-meteorological-year climate normals for each city in 5 K bins and scaled to 8,760 h. Not station data and not a design basis. Replace with a named station's TMY or bin data for design.

Result
Facility supply required at the CDU— °C
Ambient dry-bulb at or below which dry rejection alone holds it— °C
Hours per year at or below that ambient— h
Share of the year on full free cooling— %
Hours needing trim (adiabatic assist, chiller or reduced load)— h
Sensitivity. Tightening the CDU approach by one kelvin is worth — hours a year in this climate at these settings. That is the number to weigh against the larger heat exchanger.
Method and limits
  • Facility supply required = secondary supply − CDU approach. Ambient threshold = facility supply − dry-cooler approach. Hours below threshold are read from the illustrative bin distribution with linear interpolation inside a bin.
  • Dry-bulb only. Adiabatic or evaporative assist, humidity, solar gain, wind, part load and redundancy staging are not modelled.
  • Approaches are single steady-state values. Real approaches vary with load, flow and fouling; use rated-load values with a fouling allowance.
  • Outputs are illustrative screening estimates, not equipment selections, guarantees or design hours. Verify with project-specific weather data, OEM limits and a rating from the equipment manufacturer.
