Why an evidence section and not a case-study section

Case studies are curated. Evidence is structured. A case study tells you a project went well. An evidence framework tells you what "went well" was measured against, and lets you apply the same test to the next vendor. We would rather be compared on that. When we have projects whose owners allow the records to be published, they will appear here in the same structure, with the same three questions answered for each boundary.

We do not manufacture components. We take responsibility for the whole thermal path, and we hand over the evidence.
