We build with partners. We stay accountable for the result.

We qualify manufacturing partners against our factory acceptance protocols and integration partners against our commissioning plans, within the scope each contract defines. Partners are listed here by name once a project with them has been delivered and they agree to be named. Until then, this page describes the relationship, not a logo wall.

Manufacturing

Skid, manifold and CDU builders

Build to our specification and FAT protocol. We witness or audit the factory test, hold the records against serial numbers, and remain the accountable integration lead for the assembled path in service.

Integration

Rack integrators and mechanical contractors

Deploy against our commissioning plan. Every field boundary they make or disturb is logged and re-tested. They get a plan that is countable; we get records that are complete.

Off-take

Heat buyers

District networks, greenhouse and aquaculture operators, industrial process users. We design the loop to their temperature window and meter the hand-off at the data-centre boundary.

Becoming a partner. Send us your FAT protocol or your commissioning checklist. We will tell you where it meets the ten-boundary structure and where it does not. That conversation is the qualification.

We do not manufacture components. We take responsibility for the whole thermal path, and we hand over the evidence.
