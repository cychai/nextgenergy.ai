Privacy notice

This site is built to collect as little as possible. Here is exactly what happens when you visit, what happens when you write to us, and how to have anything removed.

When you visit

  • Optional Google Analytics. Google Analytics uses Consent Mode and all advertising features are disabled. Until you accept, analytics storage is denied and Google receives only cookieless measurement signals. If you accept, Google may set analytics cookies such as _ga to distinguish visits. You can change your choice at any time using “Analytics choices” in the footer.
  • Hosting logs. The site is served by Cloudflare. Like any web server, Cloudflare's edge records standard request logs (IP address, requested page, browser type, timestamp) for security and operations. We do not receive these as personal data and do not use them to identify visitors. Cloudflare's own privacy policy applies to that processing.
  • Fonts and site scripts are served from this domain. The optional Google Analytics library is loaded from Google Tag Manager; no third-party font or stylesheet is loaded.
  • Images in articles under /insights are loaded from Substack's content network (substackcdn.com), because those articles are mirrored from our Substack publication. Loading an image sends a standard request, including your IP address, to Substack.
  • The turnover self-check tool stores your tick marks in your own browser (local storage) so they survive a reload. Nothing is sent to us. Clearing your browser data removes it.

When you write to us

The contact form does not submit to a server. It opens a new message in your own email client, addressed to jim.li@nextgenergy.ai, with the fields you filled in. What we receive is an ordinary email, which we keep in our company mailbox (Google Workspace) for as long as the enquiry and any resulting relationship is active. We use it to reply to you and for nothing else. We do not sell, rent or share enquiry details with third parties.

Your choices

To have an email and its contents deleted, write to jim.li@nextgenergy.ai and say so. We will confirm when it is done. If you are in the EU, UK or Canada you have the rights those jurisdictions give you over personal data, including access and erasure, and you may exercise them through the same address.

Changes

If we add a server-side form or another third-party service that processes visitor data, this page will be updated first and the change will be dated here.
