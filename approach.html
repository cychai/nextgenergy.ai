Why this and not a product list

Component vendors optimise their box. We optimise the line.

A cold-plate maker will give you thermal resistance at a stated flow. A CDU maker will give you capacity at a stated approach. A facility designer will give you a supply temperature. Each number is honest inside its own boundary. The failures happen where the boundaries meet: the approach that eats your free-cooling hours, the quick disconnect that was never leak-tested after the last server swap, the 55 °C loop that was mixed down to 30 °C before it reached the fence and became worthless to the greenhouse next door.

We do not manufacture cold plates, CDUs or manifolds. We select them, integrate them into factory-built skids where that is possible, commission the whole path against written acceptance criteria, and stay through operation. The deliverable is a system that meets its acceptance criteria and a turnover package that shows it.

We do not manufacture components. We take responsibility for the whole thermal path, and we hand over the evidence.
