Criteria that are only ours are not criteria. They are preferences.

We review draft specifications, propose language and argue about metering boundaries in the open, so that the acceptance criteria on this site converge with what the industry will eventually require.

Where we take part

Open Compute Project, Cooling Environments. We follow the liquid-cooling workstream's public drafts and comment on them through the project's review process, with a focus on commissioning evidence, coolant quality and the boundaries between rack, CDU and facility. Where a draft is silent on what a turnover package should contain, we offer language. We are participants and reviewers, not chairs or editors of any document.

Energy-reuse metrics. The metrics that make heat reuse auditable already exist: the Energy Reuse Factor (ERF) as defined in ISO/IEC 30134-6 and EN 50600-4-6, the Energy Reuse Effectiveness (ERE) published by The Green Grid as a companion to PUE, and the metering-boundary requirement in Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2024/1364 for larger EU data centres. We use them in our own reporting and we argue for them in every forum where PUE is still the only number on the slide.

Public writing. The three-clock model, the ten leak points and the metering-boundary argument were all published first as open articles and posts, and revised in public with input from operators, water-chemistry specialists and insurers. The versions on this site are the current state of that conversation, not a finished standard.

What we ask of the documents we review

  • Every performance requirement names the load level, temperature and boundary it applies at.
  • Every pressure boundary has a test, an acceptance criterion and a record owner.
  • Cleanliness and passivation are treated as separate requirements with separate evidence.
  • Heat reuse is measured at the hand-off boundary, not estimated from the IT load.
Specific working-group roles, document titles and submission dates will be listed here as they become public. We do not list contributions to drafts that are still under embargo.
