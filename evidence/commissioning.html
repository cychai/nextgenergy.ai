Evidence · Criteria

Powered on is not AI-ready. Commissioning is where the difference is written down.

A liquid-cooled hall can be powered on, pass a walk-through and still not be ready to carry a training run. The gap is a set of acceptance criteria that were agreed before the work and tested against, gate by gate.

Gate 1

Before release from the factory

  • Pressure and leak test of every skid, manifold and CDU as shipped, with serials
  • Cleanliness class of wetted surfaces, verified by sample, not by statement
  • Material certificates for elastomers and wetted metals against the named fluid
  • Factory run of controls: pump staging, valve travel, sensor calibration
Gate 2

Before the loop is filled

  • Weld map complete; every field weld inspected and logged
  • Flush to cleanliness class; passivation confirmed separately from flushing
  • All fill, drain, vent and sample ports capped, tagged and logged
  • Pressure test of the assembled loop at the agreed factor and hold time
Gate 3

Before compute is energised

  • Fluid chemistry at fill matches specification: inhibitor, pH, conductivity, biocide
  • Flow balance across racks within tolerance, recorded per branch
  • CDU approach measured at available load and extrapolated with the method stated
  • Compute-and-liquid isolation sequence executed and timed, not just reviewed
Gate 4

Before handover

  • Baselines recorded: temperatures, pressures, flows, power, at a stated load
  • Metering boundary drawn on a single-line diagram and signed
  • Every disturbance since fill closed out in the connection log
  • Turnover package assembled in the ten-boundary structure and indexed

Three principles behind the gates

Criteria are written before the work. An acceptance criterion agreed after the test is a description of what happened. One agreed before is a specification. We put the numbers in the contract, not in the closeout report.

A boundary disturbed is a boundary re-tested. The loop does not pass once. Every quick disconnect mated after the pressure test, every port opened for a sample, resets that boundary's status until it is logged again.

The boundary of every number is stated. Load level, ambient, metering point and period go into the record with the reading. A figure without them cannot be compared to anything, including to itself a year later.
