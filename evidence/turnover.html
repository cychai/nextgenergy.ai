Evidence · Records

Ten boundaries. Three questions each. That is the turnover package.

Was it tested? Against what acceptance criteria? Where is the record? Below is the minimum we hand over for a liquid-cooled deployment, boundary by boundary. If a package cannot answer all three for all ten, it is a description, not evidence.

Beyond the joints: the system-level records

The ten-boundary table covers leak risk. A complete package also carries the records that make the system's performance defensible later:

  • IT power and facility power, metered separately, with the metering boundary drawn
  • Supply and return temperature with calibrated flow on each loop boundary
  • CDU approach at rated load, with the fouling allowance stated
  • Make-up and blowdown as separate water streams where evaporative rejection is used
  • Local dry-bulb and wet-bulb, from a station you can name
  • Delivered heat, where reuse exists: flow, supply and return, contract-grade meter
  • Equipment staging, bypass position and maintenance windows, as an event log
  • Tested compute-and-liquid isolation sequence, with the test date

None of that is expensive at design time. All of it is close to impossible to reconstruct afterwards.
